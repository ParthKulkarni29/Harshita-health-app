## Brand & Style
The design system positions itself at the apex of personal health intelligence. It communicates medical-grade precision paired with the quiet luxury of an executive wellness concierge. The visual tone is atmospheric, serene, and deeply intelligent—rejecting clinical sterile whites and gamified clutter in favor of an immersive, dark-first cockpit that honors the gravity and nuance of human biological data.

Designed for performance-minded individuals, athletes, and preventative health advocates, the interface evokes calm control and technical mastery. The aesthetic unifies high-tech bio-monitoring with modern glassmorphism: layered obsidian canvases, soft luminescent ambient glows, low-opacity vitreous membranes, and refined typographic contrast. Every datum feels alive, floating effortlessly within a low-entropy sensory environment.

## Colors
The color architecture relies on a specialized dark hierarchy built to maximize data legibility while mitigating cognitive fatigue during nighttime or early-morning biometric reviews.

### Base Structural Palette
- **Canvas Base (Deep Space):** `#080D14` – Primary app backdrop, absolute foundation.
- **Surface Level 1 (Obsidian Dark):** `#0D141D` – Sectional groupings, sidebars, page sub-canvases.
- **Surface Level 2 (Charcoal Slate):** `#121B26` – Standard interactive cards, list containers.
- **Surface Level 3 (Elevated Slate):** `#172331` – Floating overlays, modals, active card states.
- **Surface Translucent:** `rgba(18, 27, 38, 0.65)` with backdrop-filter blur.

### Typography & Monochromatic Hierarchy
- **Text High-Contrast:** `#F8FAFC` – Metrics, major headings, key callouts.
- **Text Balanced Secondary:** `#94A3B8` – Section captions, metadata, axis labels.
- **Text De-emphasized:** `#64748B` – Disabled inputs, tertiary metadata, chart gridlines.
- **Surface Stroke:** `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.08)` – Hairline borders on glass containers.
- **Surface Highlight Stroke:** `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.12)` – Hover/focus border highlights.

### Functional Telemetry Domains
Specific, vibrant color spaces designate discrete physiological dimensions:
- **Fitness & Movement:** Primary `#22C55E`, Deep `#16A34A`, Bright Accent `#4ADE80`.
- **Nutrition & Fuel:** Primary `#F97316`, Deep `#EA580C`, Bright Accent `#FB923C`.
- **Hydration & Fluid Balance:** Primary `#06B6D4`, Glow Accent `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.15)`.
- **Sleep & Circadian Rhythm:** Nocturnal Indigo `#6366F1`, Glow Accent `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)`.
- **Vitals, Heart & Nervous System:** Luminous Violet `#8B5CF6`, Glow Accent `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15)`.

Domain colors must never conflict on the same component surface; each telemetry ring, sparkline, or category badge preserves strict domain ownership.

## Typography
Typography is structured around Inter's neutral, precision-engineered geometric grotesque qualities. In health telemetry, numbers are as foundational as words: `font-feature-settings: "cv02", "cv03", "cv04", "cv11", "tnum"` must be applied across all numerical readouts, sparkline hover labels, and biometric counts to prevent layout jitter during live sensor data streams.

All uppercase tracking applies strictly to `label-sm` when used for category labels or data status tags (e.g., "RESTING HR", "DEEP SLEEP", "HRV TREND"). Headlines employ subtle negative letter-spacing to tighten optical rhythm, producing an authoritative, sleek appearance reminiscent of precision instrument displays.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs a responsive, fluid grid rooted in an 8pt architectural rhythm, augmented by 4pt micro-steps for compact data readouts and pills.

- **Desktop (12 Columns, ≥1280px):** 2.5rem margins with 2rem gutters. Layout utilizes asymmetric grouping: 8 columns dedicated to core trends and timeline sparklines, 4 columns dedicated to continuous vitals telemetry and daily targets.
- **Tablet (8 Columns, 768px - 1279px):** 1.5rem margins with 1.5rem gutters. Dual-column telemetry cards stack neatly into structured paired widgets.
- **Mobile (4 Columns, <768px):** 1rem margins with 1rem gutters. Horizontal touch scrolls allow fluid traversal across multi-metric cards without vertical compression.

Component padding strictly observes internal breathing room: widget cards use `space-lg` internally, while compact dashboard pills collapse to `space-sm` vertical by `space-md` horizontal.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation in this system departs from high-opacity, directional dropshadows. In a dark, emissive instrument panel, depth is articulated through translucent layers, frosted backplates, hairline glass bevels, and atmospheric radial glows.

### Stacking Model
- **Base Canvas:** Solid `#080D14`. Pure zero-elevation background.
- **Surface Level 1:** Flat `#0D141D` with no elevation, separating functional zones.
- **Surface Level 2 (Vitreous Card):** Background `rgba(18, 27, 38, 0.70)`, backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(180%)`. Border is a hairline `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.08)`. Shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Floating Modal / Focus Overlay):** Background `rgba(23, 35, 49, 0.85)`, backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Border is `1px solid rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.14)`. Shadow: `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.70), 0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.

### Chromatic Ambient Glows
Telemetry cards harness domain-linked radial luminescence. Rather than relying on generic black shadows, active cards project an ambient, soft backlight:
- Fitness Cards: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.12)`
- Vitals / Heart Rate: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.12)`
- Sleep: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12)`
- Hydration: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)`
- Nutrition: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.12)`

## Shapes
The shape language implements modern squircle corner curvature, establishing an organic and physiological feel across all technical modules. Sharp geometric points are avoided; surfaces mirror organic fluid mechanics.

- **Micro Components (Badges, Chips, Pills):** Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for continuous, snag-free tracking capsules.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Buttons):** Standard roundedness (`0.75rem` / 12px), creating a reliable tactile target.
- **Telemetry & Metric Cards:** Squircle containers (`rounded-2xl` / 16px to `rounded-3xl` / 24px) for dashboard widgets. The inner content matches the outer radius with proportional reduction to ensure optical harmony.
- **Hero & Modal Sheets:** Expansive rounding (`rounded-3xl` / 24px to 32px) maintaining a soft, protective embrace around sensitive biological diagnostics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Domain-Driven or Universal):** Universal buttons feature a luminous white fill `#F8FAFC` with deep slate text `#080D14`, bold weight (`font-weight: 600`), and subtle ambient drop glow (`0 4px 14px rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.2)`). Domain triggers adopt the domain color (e.g., `#22C55E` for workout logging).
- **Secondary Glass Action:** Vitreous fill `rgba(23, 35, 49, 0.70)` with hairline border `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.15)` and primary text `#F8FAFC`. On hover, the border brightens to `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.3)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No background fill; muted text `#94A3B8` transitioning to `#F8FAFC` on hover with a faint hover fill of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.

### Biometric Progress Rings
- Circular SVG progress rings with background tracks rendered in `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.10)`.
- Active meter stroke uses continuous gradients mapped to domain values (e.g., `#22C55E` transitioning to `#4ADE80`). Stroke ends are rounded (`stroke-linecap: round`).
- Centered typography displays numerical metrics (`metric-md`) alongside unit descriptors (`label-sm`).

### Sparklines & Trend Visualizations
- Ultra-minimal sparklines stripped of heavy categorical axes.
- Baselines are thin lines of `#64748B` at 0.2 opacity.
- Data paths are smoothed cubic bezier curves with 2px stroke weight, accompanied by a linear vertical gradient drop below the path that fades cleanly to zero opacity at the base.
- Interactive scrubbers display a vertical hairline indicator with a glowing circular thumb and a floating glass tooltip.

### Cards & Widget Containers
- Composed of Surface Level 2 vitreous slate with a 1px border.
- Layout order: Top metadata bar (Domain icon + uppercase category label + timestamp), followed by primary numerical metric with state delta (+3.4% or -12 bpm), and terminated with the trend visualization or secondary progress meter.
- On hover, cards subtly elevate (`transform: translateY(-2px)`) with heightened border opacity and domain-matched ambient glow.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Form controls adopt Surface Level 1 background `#0D141D` recessed into cards.
- Hairline borders in `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.15)`. Focus states elevate the border to `#06B6D4` or `#22C55E` with an inner and outer diffuse focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.15)`).
- Input text defaults to `Inter` tabular figures for numerical bio-entries.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Compact metadata filters (`label-sm`), styled with glass backgrounds and muted text.
- Selected state turns the chip solid: domain-specific background with domain-accented border and high-contrast text.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Radios
- Checkboxes are squircle toggles (`rounded-md` / 6px) with dark slate background and high-visibility domain checks.
- Segmented switches feature an enclosed track in `#080D14` with a sliding glass thumb (`#172331`) that smoothly animates between timeframe ranges (Day, Week, Month, Year).
